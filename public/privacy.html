Clutched privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Clutched helps you find oysters in New Orleans. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Your location

If you allow it, your phone's location is used on your phone only, to sort restaurants by distance and show walking times. It is never sent to us or stored by us. You can say no and type or pick a place instead.

What stays on your phone

Restaurants you save and your light/dark setting are kept in your phone's browser storage. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Outside services

The map images come from OpenStreetMap, which sees the map area being loaded, like any website. Tapping "Walk there", a phone number, a menu or Instagram opens Google Maps, your dialer, the restaurant's site or Instagram, under their own privacy policies.

Children

Clutched is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
